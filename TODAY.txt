 WHAT WE HAVE BUILT SO FAR

Our project is called Glassnik. It is a small music app that shows how music
can follow each Eye-POV Experience. You open it in your browser, and it plays
music that fits the Experience you are watching or making - decided by the
Experience's Category, Place and Location, and it keeps playing while you
move through the videos inside that grouping.

---------------------------------------------------------------
THE APP ITSELF
---------------------------------------------------------------

- The app opens in the browser with four sections (tabs):

  1. "Video & experiences" - here you upload your own video clips from
     your computer. Your videos never leave your computer - nothing is
     uploaded to any server.

   2. "Sample experiences" - there are 15 ready-made example Experiences,
     like a shopping walk through Kuala Lumpur, a beach day in Phu Quoc,
     a cafe visit in Melbourne, and so on. This tab also has the "Explore
     grouping" selector: pick one Category, Place or Location and every
     Experience inside it shares ONE soundtrack - open a different
     Experience and the music keeps playing; only switching the grouping
     changes the music.

  3. "Music credits" - the full song list (all 59 tracks), which track is
     playing right now, and the honest licence status ("no usage rights
     verified yet"). If any licence later asks for the artist to be
     credited, this is the page that does it - never over the videos.

  4. "Diagnostics" - a screen that shows the details: which songs are
     loaded, whether every music file really exists on disk, and how many
     unique files we have.

- There is a music player bar that plays one song at a time for the whole
  Experience. It shows NO track name and NO artist name (Glassnik is about
  the videos, not music discovery). It has Play/Pause, Next track, the
  music source menu - "Glassnik Music" / "Spotify - future integration"
  (visible but greyed out) / "No Music" - and volume.

- The app explains itself: every time it picks a song, it tells you WHY it
  picked that song (for example, "this song belongs to the shopping
  category, your Experience is shopping"). The names of the songs itself
  are never shown over the videos - only on the Music credits tab.

---------------------------------------------------------------
HOW THE MUSIC PICKING WORKS
---------------------------------------------------------------

- When you upload videos, all clips in one Experience count as ONE music
  context. Moving from clip 1 to clip 2 does NOT restart the music - the
  same song keeps playing. The music only changes when you change the
  Experience's setup.

- The app does not choose music by guessing. It looks at the Experience's
  category (like shopping, beaches, cafes) and plays a song from that
  category's own collection. If the Experience also says a place type (like
  "city centre" or "street market") AND has a category, the category wins -
  so a shopping Experience always gets shopping songs even if its first
  video is filmed in the city centre.

- THE CITY-CENTRE FIX (kept working): one example Experience (Barcelona)
  has a place type but no category. It first checks the place type and
  plays the versatile "works anywhere" songs - 5 different ones, taking
  turns - instead of one lonely fallback track.

THE RULE FOR HOW MUSIC MUST WORK (their main explanation)

- Glassnik videos are NOT trips. Every video is one Eye-POV Experience
  recorded with smart glasses: playing basketball, riding a bicycle,
  cooking, walking through a market, shopping, visiting a museum, playing
  golf, everyday life. Some Experiences are travel, many are not.
- Music is chosen automatically from the Category, Place or Location the
  viewer is exploring. Three examples, simplified:
  1. LOCATIONS - inside Kuala Lumpur, the music keeps playing while the
     viewer scrolls through different KL videos. Moving to Bangkok
     changes the music to suit Bangkok.
  2. CATEGORIES - choosing Sports keeps one fitting soundtrack through
     all sports videos; switching to Shopping switches the soundtrack.
  3. PLACES - inside one shopping mall, the music moves with the viewer
     between every Experience there; picking a different mall changes it.
- The rule in one sentence: music continues without stopping inside one
  Category / Place / Location; it never restarts just because the next
  video opens; it changes by itself when the grouping changes; nobody
  has to pick a track by hand; and if a track finishes while the viewer
  stays inside the grouping, the next suitable track starts alone.

  Status: CONFIRMED, and now working in the app itself. ("Sample
  experiences" tab -> "Explore grouping": one soundtrack per grouping,
  no restarts inside it, one smooth change when it switches. Proven by

28 OCTOBER (EVENING) - PIXABAY LICENCE TERMS RE-CHECKED (FAQ + FULL TERMS)
---------------------------------------------------------------

- The Pixabay licence pages were read again today and the exact wording
  is now saved (and dated) in docs/licensing-and-sourcing.md:

    * Certificates: Pixabay's FAQ says "License certificates are
      available for tracks registered with YouTube's Content ID. For
      other tracks, users should keep the download link, file name, and
      the Pixabay Content License summary as proof of permitted use."
      So a certificate is NOT given with every download - it only
      exists for Content ID tracks. For everyone else, Pixabay's own
      proof recipe is exactly our evidence folder: item page + licence
      page + download record.

    * The shield icon: on a Pixabay track page, a shield icon marks a
      track registered with Content ID (the thing that causes YouTube
      claims). The verifier now looks for it on every track page and
      records it in the register's content-id column. If it is there,
      download the certificate PDF from the page and keep it.

    * Subscription use: the FAQ allows Pixabay music inside content
      published on subscription platforms "provided the music is
      embedded as part of a creative project and not distributed
      separately or claimed as your own". That is how Glassnik works:
      music only plays under an active Experience, there is no
      standalone music player, and users cannot download files.

    * The kill-switch duty: Pixabay's full Terms (updated by Pixabay on
      20 October 2026) say that if a claim about a track turns up -
      even only a threatened or potential one - you must STOP using it
      immediately, delete it from your systems, and email
      info@pixabay.com. Glassnik's per-track takedown (one catalogue
      row plus a rebuild removes one song without touching the other
      58) exists for exactly this duty.

- What changed for the register: the reviewer now also records whether
  the shield icon was on the track page, and keeps certificate.pdf in
  the evidence folder when it is. The 59-row register itself is
  unchanged and still waits for the human pass. The pool table in
  docs/licensing-and-sourcing.md was also refreshed to match the
  catalogue after today's transfers (museums 2, family 2, festivals 2,
  wellness 2, historic sites 1, rainy day 1, adventure 1).
